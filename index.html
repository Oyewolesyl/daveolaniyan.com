Bio

Dave Olaniyan

Product Builder, Software Engineer, Design Engineer, and Founder with a B.Sc. in Geography and Environmental Studies. Dave works across product management, software engineering, design engineering, UI/UX, branding, AI product development, automation, full-stack development, and creative direction.

His pattern is to learn industries deeply, understand the actual work, then build products inside them. That work has touched SaaS, AI, sustainability, real estate, affiliate commerce, consumer hardware, healthcare communication, astrology/personal insight technology, and developer tools.
